# Rappel: product one-pager

**What it is:** Drill flashcards with free spaced repetition: build or import decks, embed one in any site, and keep the review ledger in your own hands

**Who it is for:** Someone drilling a vocabulary or a fact list they chose
themselves, usually a language learner, who already has an Anki export or a
pasted word list and wants FSRS-6 scheduling without an account, a subscription
or a sync service holding their history. They will accept a plainer screen for
a ledger that is a file they can export, read and take somewhere else.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A learner drilling kana on a phone at 23:00 with the room dark,
and the same person at a laptop beside a textbook in the afternoon. The night
scene is the one that has to work: quiet surface, one large thing to read, one
obvious key.

**What done looks like:** Every card that was due in the chosen deck has been
seen and graded, and the session ends on its own summary with the next interval
named, rather than dropping the learner back at the library with a toast.
